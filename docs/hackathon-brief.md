# TTN & TTG AI Hackathon, 3–5 November 2026 (brief digest)

Source: `../TTN_TTG_AI_Hackathon_team_pack.pdf`

## The challenge
Choose **one real problem** worth improving and with enough depth for 3 days. Use AI to explore, develop, check and improve an approach. Try it on realistic work. Leave something others can use or build on, plus a next step. A production-ready solution is **not** expected. We own the methods and the planning.

## Five starting areas
1. Understanding needs and defining work
2. Implementation and confidence in AI
3. Testing and quality
4. Bugs, recurring problems and technical debt
5. Product knowledge, documentation and technical support
